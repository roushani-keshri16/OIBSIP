FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Clear, honest answers about architecture, migration, and billing.

How does NovaTech connect to existing AWS, GCP, or Azure clusters?

NovaTech deploys an ultra-lightweight, Rust-based eBPF agent into your existing Kubernetes or Linux hosts via a single declarative Helm command or Terraform module. It operates non-intrusively in user-space with near-zero overhead (< 0.5% CPU and 25MB RAM).

Will our proprietary source code or data payloads ever leave our network?

No. NovaTech’s hybrid architecture separates the control plane from the data plane. Your raw application payloads and data streams stay inside your isolated VPC. Only anonymized telemetry metadata (latency, error codes, and mesh topology) is transmitted to orchestrate routing.

Can I test NovaTech without altering our production traffic?

Yes. NovaTech supports a pure “Shadow Mode” that mirrors incoming traffic asynchronously to evaluate routing predictions and performance gains with 0% risk to production users.

What happens if an outage affects NovaTech’s own control plane?

All local mesh agents run an autonomous decentralized cache. If communication with the NovaTech global control plane is ever interrupted, your existing infrastructure continues operating at last-known optimal configuration with zero service downtime.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

You gain immediate access to all Professional tier features for 14 days without entering credit card information. If you decide not to upgrade at the end of the trial, your account automatically transfers to our free community tier.

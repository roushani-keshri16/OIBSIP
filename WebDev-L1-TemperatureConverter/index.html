View Scientific Conversion Formulas & Limits

From Celsius (°C)

  • To Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • To Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15
  • Absolute Zero: -273.15 °C

From Fahrenheit (°F)

  • To Celsius: °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9
  • To Kelvin: K = (°F - 32) × 5/9 + 273.15
  • Absolute Zero: -459.67 °F

From Kelvin (K)

  • To Celsius: °C = K - 273.15
  • To Fahrenheit: °F = (K - 273.15) × 9/5 + 32
  • Absolute Zero: 0 K

Note on Absolute Zero: Absolute zero represents the theoretical lowest possible temperature where thermodynamic entropy and molecular motion reach minimums. Temperatures below 0 K (-273.15 °C or -459.67 °F) do not physically exist in classical thermodynamics.
